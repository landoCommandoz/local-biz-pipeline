# Incubator Pitch 0004: CallPrep Pro
**Skill:** Sales Call Prep Assistant (sales:call-prep)
**Shipping platform:** Stripe Checkout
**Price point:** $29

## Product concept
Pre-call research automation that generates attendee profiles, company intelligence, and conversation roadmaps in 5 minutes. Sales reps upload prospect info and get back a formatted brief with talking points, pain point hypotheses, and meeting agenda suggestions.

## Who buys this
B2B sales reps at startups and agencies who burn 45-90 minutes researching each prospect manually. They cannot ignore this because preparation quality directly correlates with close rates, and time saved scales their pipeline capacity.

## Why it is undervalued today
Sales reps currently spend $50-150 in opportunity cost per call doing manual LinkedIn stalking, company research, and agenda planning. Professional sales intelligence tools cost $100+ monthly but still require manual synthesis. This delivers the synthesis layer at 80% of manual quality for 20% of the time investment.

## First 7-day shipping plan
Day 1: Build prospect info intake form with company name, attendee names, call type fields. Day 2: Connect research APIs for company data and LinkedIn scraping. Day 3: Create brief generation templates for different call types (discovery, demo, closing). Day 4-7: Test with 5 beta users, iterate template quality, deploy payment flow and onboarding sequence.

## Projected month 1 revenue
$500-2000 range. Assumes 20-70 paying users at $29/month. Comparable SaaS tools in sales enablement typically see 15-25% trial to paid conversion. Customer acquisition through sales community Slack groups and LinkedIn outreach to SDRs.

## Kill criteria
If fewer than 10 paying subscribers by day 30, or if average user generates fewer than 2 briefs per month, indicating low engagement and retention risk.

## Why Brewington ships this well
The yard specializes in workflow automation for revenue-generating activities, and has existing distribution channels into the sales tooling ecosystem. Research synthesis requires exactly the kind of structured data processing and template generation that Brewington builds best.
