# Incubator Pitch 0001: SEO Audit Express
**Skill:** SEO Audit Suite (searchfit-seo)
**Shipping platform:** Gumroad
**Price point:** $97

## Product concept
One-click SEO audit reports for small business websites delivered in 24 hours. Customer submits URL, receives 15-page PDF with technical fixes, content gaps, and 90-day action roadmap.

## Who buys this
Local business owners and freelance web developers who need professional SEO analysis but cannot afford $500-2000 agency audits. They want credible documentation to justify SEO investment or client recommendations.

## Why it is undervalued today
Manual SEO audits cost $500-2000 and take 1-2 weeks. Automated tools like SEMrush ($99/month) require ongoing subscriptions and technical knowledge. This delivers agency-quality analysis as a one-time purchase with plain English explanations.

## First 7-day shipping plan
Day 1: Build intake form capturing URL and business type. Day 2: Configure automated report generation with PDF templates. Day 3: Test audit pipeline on 10 sample websites. Day 4-7: Launch on Gumroad with 5 case study examples, set up email delivery system, create simple landing page with before/after samples.

## Projected month 1 revenue
$1,940-$4,850 range. Assumes 20-50 sales at $97 each. Comparable: DetailedSEO.com sells similar reports at $79-149. Local business Facebook groups show consistent demand for affordable SEO guidance.

## Kill criteria
Fewer than 15 sales in first 30 days, OR average customer satisfaction below 4.0/5.0, OR delivery time exceeds 48 hours more than twice.

## Why Brewington ships this well
The yard already handles technical analysis and report generation. Unlike generic SaaS tools, we can customize audit depth based on business type and provide actionable priorities instead of overwhelming data dumps.
