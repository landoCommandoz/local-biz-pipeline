# Incubator Pitch 0006: Email Sequence Architect
**Skill:** Email Sequence Builder (marketing:email-sequence)
**Shipping platform:** Gumroad
**Price point:** $47

## Product concept
A downloadable tool that generates complete 7-email marketing sequences with copy, timing schedules, branching logic, and performance benchmarks. Input your product, audience, and goal: get a ready-to-deploy sequence with subject lines, body copy, send delays, and A/B test variants.

## Who buys this
Marketing consultants billing $2,000-5,000 monthly retainers who currently outsource email copywriting or spend 6-8 hours crafting sequences manually. They cannot ignore saving 85% of sequence creation time while maintaining client billing rates.

## Why it is undervalued today
Freelance email copywriters charge $500-800 per sequence and deliver in 5-7 days. Marketing agencies pay $300-600 per sequence from copy shops. This tool delivers comparable quality in 15 minutes, letting consultants either pocket the margin or offer sequences as $200 upsells to existing clients.

## First 7-day shipping plan
Day 1: Build landing page with sequence samples and consultant testimonials. Day 2: Package tool with 12 sequence templates (welcome, nurture, sales, winback). Day 3: Create demo video showing 15-minute sequence build. Day 4-7: Launch to marketing Facebook groups, cold email 200 consultants, post on Product Hunt.

## Projected month 1 revenue
$1,400-4,700. Assumes 30-100 sales at $47. Conservative estimate: marketing consultants represent 50,000+ potential buyers, conversion rate 0.06-0.2% typical for productivity tools in this space.

## Kill criteria
Less than 25 sales in first 30 days or customer complaints about sequence quality requiring manual rewrites exceeding 30% of copy.

## Why Brewington ships this well
The yard specializes in turning technical capabilities into packaged tools for service providers. Email sequence building requires domain expertise in marketing psychology and conversion optimization that aligns with existing Brewington knowledge base.
