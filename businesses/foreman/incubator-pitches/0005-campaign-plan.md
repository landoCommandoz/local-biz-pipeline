# Incubator Pitch 0005: Campaign Brief Generator
**Skill:** Marketing Campaign Planner (marketing:campaign-plan)
**Shipping platform:** Gumroad
**Price point:** $49

## Product concept
A one-click generator that produces complete marketing campaign briefs including objectives, audience profiles, messaging framework, channel strategy, 8-week content calendar, and KPI tracking sheet. Buyers input their business type, budget range, and goals, then receive a professional-grade campaign document ready for execution.

## Who buys this
Marketing consultants billing $75-150/hour who need to deliver campaign plans faster, and small business owners who cannot afford $500+ agency fees but need structured marketing direction for product launches or seasonal campaigns.

## Why it is undervalued today
Marketing agencies charge $500-2000 for campaign planning that takes 8-15 hours of strategist time. Freelance consultants charge $600-1200 for similar deliverables. This tool produces the same structured output in 5 minutes for $49, letting consultants either increase margins or serve smaller clients profitably.

## First 7-day shipping plan
Day 1: Build input form capturing business type, budget, timeline, goals. Day 2: Code campaign brief template with audience segmentation logic. Day 3: Implement content calendar generator with channel-specific formatting. Day 4-5: Test with 3 different business scenarios and refine outputs. Day 6: Create Gumroad listing with sample brief screenshots. Day 7: Launch to marketing Facebook groups and consultant communities.

## Projected month 1 revenue
$1,470-$4,900 range. Assumes 30-100 sales at $49. Comparable: marketing template packs on Gumroad sell 20-80 copies monthly. Success depends on reaching consultant networks who see immediate ROI on time savings.

## Kill criteria
Under 15 sales in first 30 days or refund rate above 20%. If consultants are not buying or buyers are unsatisfied with output quality, the value proposition fails.

## Why Brewington ships this well
The yard already serves marketing consultants and has distribution channels into freelancer communities. Existing customer base understands automation value and can provide feedback on campaign brief quality.
