# Incubator Pitch 0008: LinkFix Pro
**Skill:** Broken Link Fixer (searchfit-seo:broken-links)
**Shipping platform:** Gumroad
**Price point:** $19

## Product concept
One-click broken link scanner that generates a complete audit report plus copy-paste fixes for any website. Upload your sitemap or enter your domain, get a downloadable PDF report with every broken link found and exact HTML replacements within 5 minutes.

## Who buys this
Small business owners with 10-500 page websites who know broken links hurt SEO but lack technical skills to find and fix them systematically.

## Why it is undervalued today
Manual broken link auditing costs $200-500 from SEO consultants and takes 2-3 weeks. Current tools like Screaming Frog require technical setup and produce raw data without actionable fixes. This delivers consultant-quality results with zero learning curve.

## First 7-day shipping plan
Day 1: Build web interface accepting domain input and sitemap uploads. Day 2: Integrate broken link detection with basic HTML output. Day 3: Design PDF report template with fix suggestions. Day 4-7: Test on 20 real websites, refine output formatting, create Gumroad listing with demo report.

## Projected month 1 revenue
$570-1,140 (30-60 sales). Assuming 2% conversion from 1,500 landing page visitors via SEO forums and small business Facebook groups. Comparable: Broken Link Checker browser extensions have 100k+ users at $0 price point.

## Kill criteria
Less than 15 sales in first 30 days or customer satisfaction below 4.0 stars from first 20 buyers due to accuracy issues.

## Why Brewington ships this well
The yard specializes in practical automation tools for non-technical users. This skill produces immediately actionable output that saves real money without requiring ongoing subscriptions or complex onboarding.
