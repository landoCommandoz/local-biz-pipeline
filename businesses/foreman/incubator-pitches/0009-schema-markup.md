# Incubator Pitch 0009: LocalSchema Pro
**Skill:** JSON-LD Schema Generator (searchfit-seo:schema-markup)
**Shipping platform:** Gumroad
**Price point:** $49

## Product concept
A one-click generator that creates complete LocalBusiness JSON-LD schema markup for any local business website. Users input basic business details (name, address, hours, phone) and get production-ready code that Google immediately understands for rich snippets.

## Who buys this
Local marketing consultants managing 5-50 small business clients who need to show measurable SEO improvements quickly. They cannot ignore this because local schema markup directly impacts Google My Business rankings and rich snippet appearance, which clients can see immediately.

## Why it is undervalued today
Marketing agencies currently charge $200-500 per site for "technical SEO optimization" that includes schema markup. Most use expensive tools like Schema App ($49/month minimum) or hand-code it (3+ hours per site). This delivers the same outcome in 5 minutes at a fraction of the cost, letting consultants keep higher margins or undercut competitors.

## First 7-day shipping plan
Day 1: Build form interface for business data input (name, address, phone, hours, categories). Day 2: Wire schema generator to output valid LocalBusiness JSON-LD with proper formatting. Day 3: Add validation and preview functionality so users can verify output before download. Day 4-7: Create landing page with before/after examples, package for Gumroad with clear installation instructions, test with 3 local businesses.

## Projected month 1 revenue
$1,470-$2,940 range. Assumes 30-60 sales at $49 each. Based on comparable tools like Schema Markup Generator ($39, ~50 sales/month on similar platforms) and local SEO consultants being an active buyer segment on Gumroad.

## Kill criteria
Less than 20 sales in first 30 days or negative feedback about code quality/Google validation errors in more than 10% of reviews.

## Why Brewington ships this well
The yard already has proven SEO tool distribution through Gumroad and understands the local marketing consultant buyer persona. Technical execution is straightforward with existing JSON-LD generation capabilities.
