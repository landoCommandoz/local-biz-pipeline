# Incubator Pitch 0003: SalesBattle Generator
**Skill:** Competitive Intelligence Battlecard (sales:competitive-intelligence)
**Shipping platform:** Gumroad
**Price point:** $47

## Product concept
One-click generator that researches any competitor and outputs a professional HTML battlecard with feature comparisons, pricing analysis, and objection handling scripts. Sales reps input competitor name and their product, get a shareable battlecard in 10 minutes instead of 3-5 hours of manual research.

## Who buys this
B2B sales managers at SaaS companies who need competitive intelligence fast. Current process involves analysts spending hours researching competitors, creating PowerPoint decks, and updating quarterly.

## Why it is undervalued today
Sales teams currently pay competitive intelligence firms $30-100 per battlecard with 2-week turnaround. Manual creation takes 3-5 hours per competitor. This automation delivers equivalent quality in 10 minutes at $47 one-time cost. ROI is immediate on first use.

## First 7-day shipping plan
Day 1: Build competitor research pipeline using public data sources. Day 2: Create HTML template with feature matrix and pricing comparison sections. Day 3: Add objection handling script generation based on competitor weaknesses. Day 4-7: Package as Gumroad download with sample battlecards for Slack vs Teams, HubSpot vs Salesforce.

## Projected month 1 revenue
$2,350-$4,700 (50-100 units). Assuming 10% conversion from 500-1000 sales manager visits. Comparable: competitive intelligence templates on Gumroad sell 20-80 units monthly at similar price points.

## Kill criteria
Less than 25 sales in 30 days or customer complaints about data accuracy exceeding 15%. Both indicate product-market fit failure.

## Why Brewington ships this well
Yard specializes in automation tools for business processes. Existing customer base includes sales consultants who would promote this to their networks.
