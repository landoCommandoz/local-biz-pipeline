# Incubator Pitch 0010: SEO Cluster Generator
**Skill:** Keyword Clustering Tool (searchfit-seo:keyword-clustering)
**Shipping platform:** Gumroad
**Price point:** $39

## Product concept
One-click tool that takes a keyword list and outputs organized topic clusters with content brief templates. Upload CSV of keywords, download content calendar and topic map for 6 months of blog posts.

## Who buys this
Marketing consultants and small agency owners who charge clients $2000+ for content strategies but spend 8 hours manually clustering keywords in spreadsheets.

## Why it is undervalued today
SEO agencies charge $500-800 for keyword clustering workshops that take 6-8 hours of manual work. Current tools like Ahrefs require $99/month subscriptions plus manual interpretation. This delivers the same outcome in 10 minutes for a one-time $39 payment.

## First 7-day shipping plan
Day 1: Build CSV upload interface and basic clustering algorithm. Day 2: Create content brief templates and topic map output. Day 3: Add export functionality for popular formats (Excel, Google Sheets, Notion). Day 4-7: Test with 20 real keyword lists, polish UI, write sales copy, launch on Gumroad with demo video.

## Projected month 1 revenue
$1,560-$3,900 (40-100 sales). Assumption: 2% conversion rate from 2,000-5,000 visitors driven by SEO Twitter, marketing Slack communities, and one ProductHunt launch. Comparable: keyword research tools on Gumroad average 50-150 sales in month 1.

## Kill criteria
Less than 20 sales in first 30 days or customer satisfaction below 4.0 stars with meaningful feedback volume (10+ reviews).

## Why Brewington ships this well
The yard already has Claude model access and proven track record shipping developer tools. SEO consultants are desperate for automation that maintains quality output, perfect fit for our technical execution speed.
