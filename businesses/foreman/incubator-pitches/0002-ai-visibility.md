# Incubator Pitch 0002: ChatGPT Brand Audit Kit
**Skill:** AI Visibility Check (searchfit-seo:ai-visibility)
**Shipping platform:** Gumroad
**Price point:** $47

## Product concept
A downloadable audit kit that analyzes how your brand appears when someone asks ChatGPT, Claude, or Perplexity about your industry. Includes custom prompts, scoring rubric, and specific content fixes to improve your mentions.

## Who buys this
SaaS founders and marketing consultants who need to prove their brand exists in the new search paradigm. They cannot ignore this because their competitors are already gaming model training data.

## Why it is undervalued today
Manual brand monitoring across four major models takes 6-8 hours per audit and costs $800-1200 when outsourced to agencies. Our automated analysis delivers the same insights in 20 minutes for under $50, with specific improvement recommendations instead of vague reports.

## First 7-day shipping plan
Day 1: Build prompt library for 15 common industry queries. Day 2: Create automated scoring system for brand mention quality. Day 3: Design downloadable PDF template with action items. Day 4-7: Test with 5 beta customers and refine based on feedback.

## Projected month 1 revenue
$2,800-$4,700 (60-100 sales at $47). Assumes 2% conversion from 3,000-5,000 targeted visitors through founder Twitter and marketing consultant communities.

## Kill criteria
Less than 20 sales in first 30 days or average customer satisfaction below 4.0/5.0 stars indicates insufficient market demand.

## Why Brewington ships this well
Direct access to all major language models for real-time testing gives us distribution advantage over consultants who manually check responses.
