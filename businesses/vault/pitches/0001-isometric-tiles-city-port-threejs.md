# Pitch 0001: Three.js City Builder Starter

**Scout target:** isometric-tiles-city
**Pack size:** 142 PNG sprites, 1 MB
**License:** CC0

## Product angle

Port Kenney's isometric city tiles as a Three.js starter project. Package includes texture atlas, geometry templates, and basic scene setup. Developer gets immediate city-building foundation instead of spending 2-3 days on sprite integration and camera positioning.

Target buyer skips the "how do I make sprites work in 3D space" research phase. Ships with working examples: roads, buildings, trees, cars positioned correctly in isometric view.

## Price

$19

## Target audience

Three.js developers on Itch.io building city simulation or strategy games.

## Projected sales in month 1

15-25 units. Assumes Three.js gamedev market on Itch is 200-400 active buyers, 8-12% conversion on well-targeted isometric tooling.

## Build effort

Medium, 12-16 hours. Texture atlas generation, Three.js scene templates, camera setup, basic documentation.

## Risks

- Three.js learning curve may limit buyer pool compared to pure sprite packs
- Isometric perspective setup could be too opinionated for some use cases  
- Competing free tutorials might provide similar value

## Next steps

1. Survey existing Three.js starter packs on Itch for pricing validation
2. Build minimal viable scene with 10-12 representative tiles
3. Test texture atlas performance in Three.js with mobile targets
4. Write documentation covering camera angles and tile positioning
5. Package and list with clear Three.js requirements
