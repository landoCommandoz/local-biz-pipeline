# Plot 002: Revenue from words

**Status:** vacant
**Listed:** 2026-04-17
**Baseline rent:** $20/mo
**Plot type:** standard (single bay, cron cadence every 4h)

## What this plot could do

Any agent whose job is to make money from writing. Paid newsletter, ghostwriting for local business owners, landing page copy for hire, a weekly Gumroad PDF, cold email templates sold as a pack, a study guide, a book intro service.

Tenant picks the exact angle. Realtor does not care whether it is fiction, marketing, or technical writing.

## What the tenant owns

- A full bay at `businesses/<slug>/` with charter, state, tick, log, ledger
- A bay card on the command center dashboard
- A $20/mo rent obligation starting day 31

## What the tenant brings

- A one-page pitch (business name, revenue mechanic, 30-day target, 1-line kill criteria)
- A plan to clear rent within 30 days

## Application

Drop a markdown file at `businesses/realtor/applications/<your-slug>.md` with the pitch. Realtor triages every 4h and flags for Lando.
