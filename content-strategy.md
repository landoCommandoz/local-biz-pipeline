# 30-Day Content Strategy for Brewington Digital

## Goal
Position Lando as someone who understands the trades, not a generic marketing guy. Every post drives toward a text or email conversation. No phone calls. No ad spend. Just consistent content that makes service business owners think "this guy gets it."

---

## Platforms

### Primary: Facebook (Personal Profile + Groups)
Local service business owners live on Facebook. They scroll between jobs. They check it in the truck. This is where you reach them.

### Secondary: LinkedIn
More business-focused conversations. Good for connecting with business owners who are thinking about growth, not just surviving. Also reaches referral partners like accountants and insurance agents.

### Tertiary: Google Business Profile Posts
Helps with SEO and shows up when people search for you. Repurpose your best Facebook/LinkedIn content here.

---

## Content Pillars (Rotate Through These)

### Pillar 1: The Invisible Problem
Content about things service business owners do not realize they are losing. Missed calls. Bad websites. No reviews. Low Google visibility. The goal is to make them check their own business after reading your post.

### Pillar 2: The System
Content about how smart business owners automate the stuff that steals their time. Text-back systems, automated reviews, online booking. Show how the system works without pitching your specific product.

### Pillar 3: Real Talk from the Trades
Content that proves you understand what it is like to run a service business. The frustration of customers not answering when you call back. The guy who wants a $50 fix on a $500 problem. Slow seasons. Price shoppers. Speak their language.

### Pillar 4: Proof and Results
Before/afters. Screenshots. Data. "Here is what happened when a plumber went from 12 reviews to 80." Show, do not tell. Even if the proof is from a demo site or a business you helped for free.

### Pillar 5: Lando's Story
Occasional personal posts. Not every day. But enough that people know who you are, where you came from, and why you are doing this. People buy from people they feel connected to.

## Engagement Rules

### When Someone Comments
Reply to every comment within 2 hours. Ask a follow-up question. Keep the conversation going in public, then move to DM if they show interest.

### When Someone DMs You
Respond within 1 hour during business hours. Ask about their specific business. Do not pitch immediately. Understand their situation first, then offer to help.

### How to Turn a Comment into a Lead
1. They comment on your post
2. You reply with a helpful response + a question
3. They reply again
4. You DM them: "Hey, did not want to hijack the comment thread. Based on what you said, I think I can help. What is the best way to reach you, text or email?"

## Repurposing Content

Every Facebook post can be:
1. Copy-pasted to LinkedIn with minor tone adjustment (more professional, less casual)
2. Shortened for a GBP post
3. Turned into a Reddit comment when relevant
4. Screenshot'd and posted as an image on other platforms
5. Expanded into a longer email for your outreach list

One piece of content, five uses. Write once, post everywhere.
