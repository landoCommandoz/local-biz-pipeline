After the scan + CTA, what happens to the page?

The visitor just entered their URL, watched the scan, and either gave you their email, texted you, or skipped. Now what do they see?

A

Scan results stay, site continues below

The scan report stays visible in the hero as a persistent "your results" card. As they scroll, the rest of the site (proof bar, live system, features, pricing, etc.) flows normally underneath. Their scan results are always there if they scroll back up. The site now feels personalized — they gave you their URL and the page responded.

Natural flow. Nothing hidden. Site sells while their results linger.

B

Scan fades, original hero content appears

After the CTA moment (email captured or skipped), the scan terminal gracefully fades and the original hero copy reveals: "Your phone is ringing. Nobody's there." + proof strip + CTAs. Now the site flows exactly as designed. The scan was the opener, the site is the closer.

Preserves the GOLD hero we already built. Scan is the hook, hero is the punch.

C

Auto-scroll past hero into the site

After the CTA moment, the page smoothly auto-scrolls down to the proof bar. The scan did its job — now the site takes over. If they scroll back up, they see a compact summary of their scan results (collapsed state). Clean transition from "your problem" to "our solution."

Momentum. Keeps them moving through the funnel. No dead air.
