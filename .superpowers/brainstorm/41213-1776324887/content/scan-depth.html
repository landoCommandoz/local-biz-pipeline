How deep does the on-site scan feel?

The scan is simulated but needs to feel real. How far do we take it?

A

Terminal Only (current style, personalized)

The diagnostic terminal runs like it does now, but uses their URL in the command line: $ brewington --scan joesplumbing.com. Same categories (missed calls, online presence, website, revenue leak), but their business name appears in the results. Everything else is templated.

Fast to build. Keeps Hero structure. Feels personalized enough.

B

Terminal + Visual Report Card

Terminal runs the scan, then the right side TRANSFORMS into a visual report card. Animated gauges, color-coded scores (red/yellow/green), review analysis section, revenue leak breakdown with their name on it. Like going from CLI to dashboard.

More impressive. The transformation moment is dramatic. More to build.

C

Full Page Takeover

After the scan, the ENTIRE page below the nav becomes their report. Sections scroll through: Review Analysis → Website Score → Missed Call Estimate → Competitor Comparison → "Here's how we fix it" → CTA. The normal site content is hidden until they dismiss the report.

Maximum impact. Feels like a real product. Most complex to build.

REVIEW ANALYSIS (included in B and C)

The review section would show:

  • "Your Google rating: ★★★☆☆ (3.2)" with gauge
  • "12 total reviews — 4 are below 3 stars"
  • Simulated bad review quote: "Called twice, nobody answered. Went with someone else."
  • Revenue impact: "Each 1-star review costs ~$750/year in lost business"
  • Competitor comparison: "Top competitor: 4.8 stars, 89 reviews"

Note: On-site version uses smart templates. The real data comes in the follow-up email audit.
