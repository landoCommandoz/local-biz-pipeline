Where does the URL input live?

Where the visitor enters their website to trigger the scan

A

Replace the Hero H1

The URL input IS the hero. Instead of "Your phone is ringing. Nobody's there." they see a command-line style input: Enter your website to scan. After they submit, the terminal on the right runs the scan using their URL. The H1 and copy appear after the scan completes.

Pros

  • Immediate engagement, no scrolling
  • The terminal becomes interactive, not decorative
  • Strongest lead capture — they act before reading

Cons

  • We just got Hero to GOLD. This changes it.
  • Visitors who don't enter a URL see... what?
B

Modal / Overlay on Landing

A sleek, dark modal appears 2-3 seconds after page load (or immediately). Minimal: just an input field, a "Scan My Business" button, and a "Skip" link. After submission, the modal closes and the hero terminal runs their scan. Skip dismisses it and shows the normal site.

Pros

  • Hero stays GOLD / untouched
  • Focused moment — nothing else competing for attention
  • Skip option means no one is trapped

Cons

  • Popups can feel aggressive
  • Some visitors will skip without thinking
C

Integrated Into the Terminal

The hero terminal starts with a blinking cursor and the line: $ enter your website to begin scan_. When they type/paste a URL and hit enter, the scan runs right there inside the terminal. If they don't interact, the default scan auto-plays after 8 seconds.

Pros

  • Feels native to the command-center aesthetic
  • Hero stays mostly intact — just the terminal changes
  • Auto-fallback means passive visitors still see the show

Cons

  • Input inside a fake terminal may confuse some users
  • Mobile typing in a small terminal area could be awkward
D

New Section Between Hero and Proof Bar

A dedicated "Scan Your Business" section sits right after the hero. Full-width, dark, with a large input field and a dramatic CTA. After submission, it expands into a visual report showing their scan results with animated gauges, scores, and recommendations.

Pros

  • Hero completely untouched
  • Plenty of space for the input + results
  • Natural scroll progression: hook → scan → proof

Cons

  • Adds a new section to rate and build
  • Visitor has to scroll past the hero first
