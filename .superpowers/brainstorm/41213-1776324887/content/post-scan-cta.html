The scan just finished. Now what?

They're staring at their "results." This is the highest-intent moment on the entire page. What do you ask for?

A

Text to get the full report

Scan ends with: "We found 7 problems costing you ~$8,750/mo. Want the full breakdown?" → Text "FIX" to 602-609-4756. They text, you now have their phone number AND their website. The deep audit email follows within 24 hours.

Lowest friction. Matches your existing CTA. Gets the phone number which is gold for a contractor audience.

B

Email to get the full report

Scan ends with an email input: "Enter your email for the full audit report — sent within 24 hours." Clean input field + submit button right there. Now you have URL + email. The deep audit arrives in their inbox.

Captures email for nurture sequences. Less personal than texting but more scalable.

C

Both — email for the report, text to talk now

Two paths after scan:
Primary: Email input for the full audit report
Secondary: "Want to fix this today? Text 602-609-4756"

Captures the tire-kickers (email) and the ready-to-buy (text) separately.

Catches both temperature levels. More complex UI but maximum capture.

D

Dismiss and let the site sell

Scan ends, results stay visible for a moment, then the report fades and the normal site continues below. The scan planted the seed — now the proof bar, testimonials, pricing, and founder sections do the closing. No extra input asked.

Least aggressive. Trusts the rest of the site. But you lose the lead if they bounce.
